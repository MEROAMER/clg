## Definitions

1. SWE: Engineering discipline concerned with all aspects of sw production from system specifications to maintaining sys after it's gone to use.
	- Engineering discipline: using appropriate theories/methods to solve problems; keeping in mind org/financial constraints.
	- all aspects of sw production: not just tech process of dev, also proj mng & dev of tools, methods, etc to support production.

2. what's software?
	- computer programs + associated documentation.
	- maybe dev for: particular customer || general market.

3. what's a GOOD SW? 
	- deliver required functionality + performance => to user
	- should be: maintainable + dependable + usable
